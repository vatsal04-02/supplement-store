Product checks

Verify before you use

Packaging and verification methods vary by manufacturer. Use the product label and the brand’s own guidance for the item you are considering.

01
Inspect the sealed packaging

Check that the cap, inner seal and outer packaging are intact before opening. Do not use a product if the seal appears damaged.

02
Match batch and date details

Read the batch or lot number, manufacturing date and expiry date printed on the pack. Ask the store before ordering if these details are important to your choice.

03
Follow the manufacturer’s process

If the brand provides a QR code, scratch code or official verification channel, follow the instructions printed on that product’s packaging and use the manufacturer’s official destination.

04
Keep the invoice and packaging

Ask the store what billing document is available and retain it with the original container if you need to contact the manufacturer or store.
