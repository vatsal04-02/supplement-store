How each number is counted

Visits
Distinct browser-tab sessions that sent at least one event. A session ends when the tab is closed.
Visitors
Anonymous visitors counted per day, then added across the range. Someone who visits on three days counts three times; people cannot be followed across days by design.
Page views
Storefront page loads.
Product detail views
Times a product details dialog was opened.
Units added
Units added to order lists, including quantity increases inside the list. Removals are shown separately in the products table.
WhatsApp taps
Taps on any WhatsApp link or button. WhatsApp then opens on the customer’s device; whether a message was actually sent cannot be measured, so this is not a count of enquiries or orders.
Phone taps
Taps on a telephone link. Whether a call was placed cannot be measured.
Journey rates
“Opened product details” and “Opened a WhatsApp enquiry” are shares of all visits; “Added to the order list” is a share of visits that opened product details.
Searches
Catalogue searches of two or more characters, recorded after typing pauses. E-mail addresses and long numbers are removed before storage.
Not counted
Visitors who switched statistics off, browsers sending Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, known bots, and visits where JavaScript did not run. Totals are therefore a lower bound.
